Development and Application of BIM-Based Virtual Reality Support Tools in MiC Projects.

Exploring construction decisions in VR

This dissertation explored BIM-based virtual reality support for modular integrated construction (MiC), with attention to lifting-path planning, spatial coordination and site logistics.

I developed the support concept and an interactive VR prototype, and analysed qualitative practitioner feedback to examine the proposed support for construction work.

My contribution

Individual dissertation research

  • Developed the BIM–VR support concept for MiC.
  • Implemented an interactive VR prototype.
  • Analysed qualitative practitioner feedback.
  • Produced the master’s dissertation.

Practitioner feedback informed the research; no measured productivity or deployment outcome is claimed here.

The completed work includes a dissertation and an interactive VR prototype. It forms an earlier part of my work on digital tools for professional decision support, alongside my later research on evidence-grounded AI.
