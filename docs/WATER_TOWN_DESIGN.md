## Final background prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: full-bleed background image for a real interactive GitHub Pages personal portfolio; the HTML/CSS interface will be built separately. Generate ONE uninterrupted wide landscape scene, 16:9, high resolution, ideally 3840 by 2160. Scene: a photorealistic Jiangnan water-town canal with dark-tiled wooden houses lining both banks, cascading purple wisteria blossoms hanging from eaves and overhead branches, glowing warm round lanterns, lush green trees, and a traditional black-canopy wooden boat floating on calm reflective water. The canal and water reflections form the main scene; tiled timber architecture, wisteria, lanterns, greenery and the boat must all be clearly recognizable. Style: strictly photorealistic, high-end 3D game environment realism, physically based materials and cinematic global illumination, weathered wood grain, individual dark roof tiles, detailed purple flowers, natural foliage, gentle realistic ripples and warm reflected lantern light. Atmospheric early evening / blue hour, deep charcoal and jade shadows, warm amber lanterns, subtle lavender wisteria; dark but retain richly visible scenery and natural tonal detail. Composition: eye-level view along the canal, spacious and breathable, a calm central water corridor with natural depth, flowers framing the top, houses along the sides, black-canopy boat toward the lower right so the left half can support overlaid website introduction text. Keep the lower water and main foliage coherent under a centered desktop crop. Output only the landscape background: no browser frame, no device, no UI, no panels, no text, no lettering, no stamp, no logo, no watermark, no borders, no collage or split panels. This must be a realistic scenic environment, never 2D ink painting, watercolor, illustration, anime, flat vector art or an impressionistic image.
