Contribution and project evidence

RESEARCH SUMMARY

Evidence-grounded human–AI pre-review for construction tendering.

Question. How can a pre-review record bind dispersed tender excerpts and candidate rules to inspectable professional dispositions, while separating supported findings from unresolved evidence gaps and post-processing shifts?

Approach. A task-bound program pairs ordered regulatory retrieval (L1–L4) and source-bound observation with structured reasoning. Deterministic release gates verify source locators, evidence eligibility, and strict gap conditions before releasing or holding records. Domain practitioners then appraise delivered records across five dimensions and record formal dispositions and follow-up rationales.

Evidence. On a previously unseen construction project (IPV01), the program delivered 24 of 30 selected tasks. Both domain evaluators passed source traceability on 23 of 24 delivered records, while accepting 21 and 16 records respectively and pinpointing targeted revisions. Case analyses (IPV-U10, IPV-U14, IPV-U02/U22) further establish requirements for cross-stage judgment-preservation checks and structured continuation records.

The IPV01 results above are a study summary. Task-level IPV01 records are not included in the public repository; the demonstrations below use separate synthetic data and fixtures.
